Data Deletion Request for Pizza Paradize

We are committed to protecting your privacy. You can request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request the deletion of your data, please follow these steps:

  1. Compose an email to our support team at Mukundsanjeevi8@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request".
  3. In the body of the email, please include the phone number you used to place your orders. This is required to identify your account and associated data.

Once we receive your request, we will verify your information and proceed with the deletion process. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete the following personally identifiable information associated with your account:

  • Your Name
  • Your Phone Number
  • Your Delivery Address(es)
  • Your complete Order History

Data Retention

Please note that we may retain anonymized and aggregated data for analytical and statistical purposes. This data cannot be linked back to any individual and does not contain any personal information.

The deletion process will be completed within 30 days of receiving your request.
