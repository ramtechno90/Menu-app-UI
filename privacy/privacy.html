Privacy Policy for Pizza Paradize

This privacy policy applies to the Pizza Paradize mobile application (the "App"), the official app for the Pizza Paradize restaurant. It governs the privacy of its users who choose to use it and explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect two types of information: information you provide directly to us, and technical information collected automatically to ensure the App works correctly.

1. Information You Provide to Us

  • Name: To personalize your experience.
  • Delivery Address: To deliver your order correctly.
  • Phone Number: To contact you regarding your order. Please note, your phone number will only be used for communication related to your order and delivery.
  • Order Details: The specific food items, quantities, and special instructions you select for your purchase.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

When you use the App, we collect certain technical data to improve the App's user experience and features, and to help us fix bugs and improve performance. This data is not linked to your personal identity (like your name) but is associated with a Non-Personal ID (which is generated randomly).

  • Crash Reports & Performance Data: Technical logs that tell us when and why the App crashed or froze.
  • App Usage & Event Logs: Data on how you interact with the App, for example which menu screens you visit, when you add items to the cart, and which search filters you use.
  • Device Information: Basic details about your device model and operating system version, which helps us troubleshoot issues specific to certain phones.

How We Keep Your Session Secure

To keep track of your cart and orders, our App uses a secure, random session identifier (provided by Firebase Anonymous Authentication). This identifier is generated randomly and does not inherently contain your personal identity or hardware details. It allows us to securely maintain your session on your device without requiring you to create a password. We use this identifier solely to associate the order details you provide (for example your name, phone number, and address) with your current session so we can fulfill your request.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect primarily to fulfill your food orders and ensure the App runs smoothly. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Process and Deliver Orders: To prepare your food and ensure it reaches the correct delivery address.
  • Order Communication: To contact you specifically regarding order status, delivery updates, or customer support queries. We strictly do not use your contact details for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • App Performance & Stability: To monitor the health of our App, we use an automated crash reporting tool to collect crash reports and performance data, which helps us identify bugs and fix technical issues to improve your experience.
  • User Flow Analysis: To identify where users might face difficulties so we can streamline the purchasing process, we log specific technical events (such as viewing the menu, adding items to the cart, or completing an order). This data is linked to a Non-Personal ID rather than your personal identity.
  • Feature Optimization: To decide which areas of the App to improve, we track feature usage patterns (e.g., how often users search for a particular dish or particular food category). This helps us prioritize updates that matter most to you. This usage data is associated with a Non-Personal ID instead of your personal identity.
  • Technical Diagnostics: To solve complex technical bugs, we analyze the sequence of events that occurred right before a crash. This analysis relies on data linked only to a Non-Personal ID, allowing us to trace the technical steps causing the error without viewing your personal information.
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent transactions or abuse of our services.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our App, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

Security

We employ custom logic and custom security measures designed to fit the specific functional requirements of this App. We have implemented these measures to the best of our knowledge and effort to safeguard the App and your data against hackers, unauthorized access, and similar malicious activities. However, please be aware that no application is entirely immune to risks. We cannot guarantee that the App is hack-proof, especially if your mobile device itself is compromised, rooted, or infected with malware. By using this App, you acknowledge that the software is provided "as-is" and while we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security against hackers, reverse engineering, or unauthorized third-party access.

Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you learn that your child has provided us with Personal Information, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Information from a child under age 13 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information from our storage database.
